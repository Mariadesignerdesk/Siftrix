## 3. Tiered Advisory Playbooks

### A. Low Risk (Score: 0.0 – 29.9 | Safe)
* **Action Required:** No immediate threat detected. Exercise routine cyber caution.
* **Dos:**
  - Verify unprompted messages through independent, official channels.
  - Keep device operating systems and mobile banking apps updated.
* **Donts:**
  - Do not share sensitive personal information or passwords with unknown senders.

### B. Medium Risk (Score: 30.0 – 59.9 | Suspicious)
* **Action Required:** Treat message or link with high caution. Do not click links or share details.
* **Dos:**
  - Verify sender identity by calling the organization via their official published number.
  - Report suspicious SMS to PTA by forwarding sender number and content to **9000**.
* **Donts:**
  - Do not click links or download attachments from this message.
  - Never share OTPs, PINs, or CNIC details under any circumstances.

### C. High Risk (Score: 60.0 – 100.0 | High)
* **Action Required:** Critical threat detected. Immediate containment and reporting required.
* **Dos:**
  - If financial details were shared, call your bank immediately using the phone number printed on the back of your card to freeze accounts.
  - File an online cybercrime complaint with NCCIA at **https://complaint.nccia.gov.pk/** or call **1799**.
  - Block the sender number on your device and report fraudulent SMS to PTA via **9000**.
* **Donts:**
  - Do NOT click any links, open attachments, or reply to the sender.
  - Never provide verification codes, banking passwords, or transfer advance fees.

## 4. Bilingual Localization Standards (English & Roman Urdu)

### High Empathy Phrasing for Victims:
* **English:** "Stay calm. If you have not shared passwords or clicked the link, your account remains secure."
* **Roman Urdu:** "Ghabranay ki zaroorat nahi hai. Agar aap ne abhi tak koi OTP ya password share nahi kiya to aap ka account mehfooz hai."

### Phishing Explanation:
* **English:** "This message attempts to impersonate an official institution to steal your confidential credentials."
* **Roman Urdu:** "Yeh paighaam ek farzi idaray ki taraf se aap ki zati maloomat aur password churanay ke liye bheja gaya hai."
